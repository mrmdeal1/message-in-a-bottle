Vicissitude Support

Help with accounts, bottle purchases, journeys, safety, and app use.

Getting Help

If you need help with Vicissitude or need to report inappropriate activity, contact us at vicissitudesupport@agentmail.to.

Safety & User-Generated Content

Vicissitude is intended for adults age 18 and older. By creating an account and using Vicissitude, users agree that objectionable content and abusive behavior are not permitted.

Vicissitude has zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Messages are subject to automated safety filtering. Users can report a message and block the sender directly from the opened-bottle screen.

Reported content is removed from distribution immediately. The reported account may be suspended pending review. Reports are reviewed and acted on within 24 hours.

Accounts

Use the same username and password you created in Vicissitude to sign in. Your bottle history is tied to your account.

Monthly Bottle Purchase

Vicissitude allows one bottle purchase per account per calendar month. Purchases are completed through Apple In-App Purchase.

Bottle Journeys

After purchase, write a message, seal the bottle, choose a launch area, and throw it. Journeys are influenced by real ocean-current data and simulated hazards and conditions. A bottle may continue drifting, wash ashore, be found, be opened, be thrown back unopened, be lost at sea, destroyed, or buried on the beach.

Replies

If another user opens your bottle, they may send one reply. Once a bottle is opened, that bottle's journey permanently ends.

Purchase Problems

If an Apple purchase completes but your bottle does not become available, close and reopen Vicissitude, confirm you are signed into the same Vicissitude account, and contact support if the issue continues.
